## 申诉正文（英文，可直接粘贴）

**Subject:** Reconsideration — VLStream (now "VLStream – Camera Viewer") — Guideline 1.1.6 (Submission 4bfebf9-5f89-4772-a36a-be9056848411)

Dear App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing VLStream (version 1.0). We appreciate the feedback under Guideline 1.1.6 – Safety – Objectionable Content, which noted that the app's name could be read as a consumer media-streaming app.

To remove any ambiguity, we have updated the App Store name to **"VLStream – Camera Viewer"**, which clearly conveys that this is a camera/device video client rather than a movie, music, or entertainment media service.

We would also like to clarify what the product actually is, so the name is understood in context:

- "VL" is our product-line prefix, and "Stream" refers specifically to the **real-time streaming of live video from the user's own connected cameras and devices** — the app's core and only media function. It does not relate to any entertainment media catalog.
- VLStream is a **professional IoT / device-management client**. Its primary feature is displaying live video feeds from authorized cameras and devices, together with map & trajectory playback, event management, an organizational contacts tree, and form-based field operations.
- It is an **authorized-user / enterprise tool**: it requires account login against a private backend and streams only the devices the signed-in user is permitted to access. There is no public content catalog, no subscription media library, and no consumer entertainment offering.
- The app delivers exactly what its name promises — streaming live video from connected devices — and nothing it implies is absent or deceptive.

We have no intent to mislead or deceive users. The app metadata and the in-app experience are consistent with its genuine purpose. We respectfully ask the team to reconsider the rejection.

If it would help, we are happy to provide a demo account or screen recordings demonstrating the real functionality — live device video streaming plus the associated IoT features — so the review team can verify the app matches its name.

Thank you for your time and for helping us improve the submission.

Best regards,

## 两个提交路径提醒

1. **走「提交复核」+ 备注**：最常见做法——在 App Store Connect 把名字改好后，点「提交复核」，并把上面正文（或精简版）粘到「备注 / Notes」字段。无需走正式申诉流程。
2. **走 App Review Board 正式申诉**：仅当你**不认同**拒审、坚持原名时才用。但你已经改名，属于「已配合整改」，走路径 1 更自然、过审概率更高。

> 无论哪条路径，都**不需要重新打包**——只要改的是 App Store Connect 元数据名称（商店页）。只有当你想把桌面图标名（Info.plist 的 CFBundleDisplayName）也一并改成 "VLStream – Camera Viewer" 时才需 rebuild + 上传新 build（build 号 +1）。
