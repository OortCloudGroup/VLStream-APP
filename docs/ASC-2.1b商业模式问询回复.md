## 英文回复（直接粘贴）

Hello Review Team,

Thank you for the questions. VLStream is a **completely free companion app** for OORT smart cameras (physical hardware manufactured and sold by our company, Aort Cloud (Shenzhen) Smart Technology Co., Ltd.). The app contains **no paid digital content, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no paywall of any kind**. Below are detailed answers to each question.

**1. Who are the users that will use the paid content, subscriptions, features, and services in the app?**

Our users are enterprise and organizational customers — for example, construction-site safety supervisors and facility security operators — who use OORT cameras purchased by their companies for workplace safety monitoring. The app itself is free for everyone: any user can download the app, create/log in to an account, and use 100% of its features at no charge. The only "paid" element in our ecosystem is the physical camera hardware, which is purchased by companies — never by end users inside the app.

**2. Where can users purchase the content, subscriptions, features, and services that can be accessed in the app?**

All purchases happen **entirely outside the app**. Physical OORT camera devices are bought directly from our company or from authorized hardware dealers through offline / enterprise procurement channels (sales contracts, purchase orders). There is no purchase flow, no payment button, no storefront, and no subscription offering anywhere inside the app. The app has no commerce functionality at all.

**3. What specific types of previously purchased content, subscriptions, features, and services can a user access in the app?**

None in the digital sense. After logging in, a user can view and manage only the physical camera devices that belong to their own account or organization: live video streams, recorded playback, and AI safety-event alerts (e.g., helmet / reflective-vest detection) generated by those cameras. Access to a device exists simply because the user's organization owns that physical device and has added it to the account. There is no digital content, subscription, or service that is purchased or unlocked.

**4. What paid content, subscriptions, or features are unlocked within the app that do not use In-App Purchase?**

None. There is no paid content, no subscription, and no feature gating in the app. Every feature — live view, playback, device management, event alerts — is free. The video content displayed in the app originates from the user's own physical cameras; we do not sell or license any content.

**5. Can users purchase physical goods or services together with digital content in your app? If so, please describe how the physical and digital content are connected and why you bundle them together in a single purchase.**

No purchases of any kind can be made inside the app — physical or digital. The app's functionality is inherently tied to our physical hardware: when a customer purchases OORT cameras through enterprise procurement, the devices are registered to the customer's account during deployment, and the app then serves as the viewing/management client for that hardware. The digital functionality is not sold — it is a free companion utility for hardware the customer already owns, in the same way a camera manufacturer's official viewing app works. Because all hardware sales and any service agreements occur entirely outside the app through enterprise channels, we believe In-App Purchase requirements (Guideline 3.1.1) are not applicable.

If it would help the review, we are happy to provide hardware purchase documentation, screenshots showing there is no purchase entry anywhere in the app, or any other materials you need. A fully functional demo account is already provided in the App Review notes.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
VLStream Team

## 中文对照（内部参考）

1. **谁在用付费内容？** 企业客户（工地安全监管、园区安防运营等），用的是公司采购的 OORT 摄像头。App 本身完全免费，任何人下载后 100% 功能免费使用。生态里唯一的"付费"是摄像头硬件，且由企业采购——绝不在 App 内购买。
2. **在哪购买？** 全部在 App 外：直接向公司或授权经销商线下/企业渠道采购。App 内没有任何购买入口、支付按钮、商店或订阅。
3. **能访问哪些已购内容？** 数字意义上没有。登录后只能查看自己账号/组织名下的物理摄像头：实时视频、回放、AI 安全事件告警。设备可见仅仅因为组织拥有这台物理设备并已绑定账号。
4. **哪些付费功能绕过了 IAP？** 没有。所有功能（实时画面、回放、设备管理、事件告警）全部免费，视频来自用户自己的摄像头，公司不出售任何内容。
5. **物理商品+数字内容捆绑销售？** App 内不存在任何购买行为。摄像头经企业采购后在部署阶段绑定到客户账号，App 只是这些硬件的免费配套查看/管理客户端（同相机厂商官方查看 App 的定位）。硬件销售与服务协议全部发生在 App 外的企业渠道，因此 3.1.1 IAP 不适用。
